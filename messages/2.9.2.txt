Algorithm Competition Assistant 2.9.2
=====================================

The test cards and the detail tab loosen up: shorter rows that no longer wrap,
and colour that stays only where the diff is.

* The cards drop "time:" and "mem:" - the values say it with their units

  A card read "test 4 | edit | run | detail | time: 2542ms | mem: 4 MB". Two
  problems came from that: the labels repeated what the ms / MB units already
  say, and the redundant width was exactly what made a slow card (2542ms in
  the time chip) spill its "4 MB" onto a second line, where it landed on top
  of the row below. The chips now read "2542ms" and "4 MB", the card keeps the
  same information in less than half the label width, and the slow-card row
  stays on one line. The same two characters go from the detail tab's plain
  header and its phantom badge bar - which was also why the copy buttons were
  the first thing clipped off the bar's right edge.

- The memory value stops shouting

  Every card and every detail bar showed the memory figure in pink; on four
  rows it reads as noise. The value is now dimmed like secondary text, and
  coloured detail remains only where it carries a verdict (the badge) or marks
  a difference (the green / red diff pair).

- The detail tab's headers stop wearing a blue slab

  The section titles and the "time used / vs last / history" facts were all
  filled with a blue background. The bracketed titles already say "header",
  and the fill fought with the two diff colours the page is actually about.
  The slab is gone; the diff lines keep theirs.

  The badge in the detail's bar also takes the verdict's own class now - the
  old hand-picked map looked up 'TL' for TLE (a name that never occurs), so a
  TLE detail showed a bare unbackgrounded verdict and every verdict outside
  its six entries fell the same way. All of them had rules in the stylesheet
  already.

Freeloaded fixes in this release: none. Regression suite: 869 -> 876 checks.
checks. The new cases pin the label removal in both card templates and in the
translation table, the badge taking the verdict's own class, the value keeping
its NBSP as a real character after escaping, and the card-width estimator
counting the label-free rows.
